The problem

Medical offices still receive referrals, lab results, insurance authorizations, and records requests by fax. Staff read and sort each one by hand — which takes time, slows patients down, and makes it easy to miss something urgent.

How it works

Four steps from fax in to appointment on the calendar

1

Fax arrives

Incoming faxes land in FaxFlow the same way they reach your office today.

2

AI reads and classifies

FaxFlow reads the document and labels it — referral, lab result, authorization, and more.

3

Details extracted

Patient details are pulled out and urgent cases are flagged for faster review.

4

Staff review and schedule

Your team works a clear queue — review, reach out, and schedule appointments.

Features

Built for busy front desks and clinical coordinators

Automatic classification

Sorts referrals, lab results, insurance authorizations, records requests, and more.

Patient data extraction

Pulls name, date of birth, MRN, and referring physician from each fax.

FAQ

What is FaxFlow?

FaxFlow is software that helps medical offices handle incoming faxes. It uses AI to read each fax, sort it by type, pull out key details, and help staff review and schedule from one queue.

Does it replace our fax machine?

No. FaxFlow works with the faxes your office already receives. It does not replace your fax line or fax machine — it helps your team process what comes in.

Does it connect to our EHR?

EHR connections are planned as part of pilot and onboarding discussions. What's available depends on your system and setup — we'll talk through that with each pilot partner.

How is patient data handled?

FaxFlow is a pilot-stage product built with healthcare privacy requirements in mind. HIPAA-compliant infrastructure and Business Associate Agreements are part of onboarding before any real patient data is processed.

How do we get started?

Use the pilot request form on this page. Tell us about your office and fax volume — without including patient information — and we'll follow up to discuss next steps.
